Do these three things now

  1. Get at least one B this fall in College Algebra or Managerial Accounting. One B keeps you above 3.0. Two C's drops you to about 2.95, and that closes Urban Sciences, PPEL and Criminology.
  2. Register for spring. Registration is open and you aren't signed up for anything yet. Take these three:
    • AMH 2020 US History since 1877, for the journalism majors
    • SPC 2608 Public Speaking, for Tourism and Hospitality
    • SYG 2000 Intro to Sociology, also for Tourism and Hospitality
    Optional fourth: EDF 1005 Intro to Teaching, which is all you need for Education Sciences.
  3. Pick your one campus major by January. UF only lets you apply to one campus major per term, and you're very unlikely to be able to switch after you get in.

Your best campus options

Ranked by how good your chances are, not by how the major sounds.

Urban Sciences and Planning

Good chances

How cities work: planning, economics, policy and data. Jobs in city planning and urban data analysis.

You have: everything it requires.

You need: to keep your GPA at 3.0 or higher.

Apply by: Feb 1 to start in summer, or May 1 to start in fall.

Education Sciences

Good chances

Education without becoming a classroom teacher. Jobs in training, education technology, policy and nonprofits.

You have: nothing yet.

You need: one class, EDF 1005 Intro to Teaching, with a C or better. It only needs a 2.0 GPA, and it doesn't cap how many students it takes.

Apply by: May 1 for fall.

Advertising, Public Relations, Journalism, or Media Production

Decent chances

Four majors in the journalism college. Advertising is the most business-like: brands, marketing and campaigns.

You have: four of the five required classes.

You need: AMH 2020 US History since 1877 this spring. The minimum GPA is 2.0, and the college says 2.5 makes you competitive, so you're above that.

Apply by: May 1 for fall.

Hard

Hotels, events and tourism businesses. Has an Entrepreneurship certificate, which fits wanting to run your own business.

Backup: UF Online

Separate from everything above. You can apply to one campus major and one online major at the same time, so an online application is a safety net if the campus one doesn't work out. It's the same UF degree, with lower GPA requirements. Online deadlines: Jan 25 to start in summer, Apr 26 to start in fall.
