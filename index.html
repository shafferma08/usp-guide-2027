What to do

Short list, in order.

Already done: AA graduation application filed ✓

  • Associate in Arts, Fall 2026. The degree posts in December, and Florida AA graduates get transfer priority at UF.

1. This fall, the only thing that really matters

  • Pass College Algebra. Not for any major directly, but because it finishes the math requirement for your AA. No AA means no transfer, for any of these options.
  • Keep your overall GPA at or above 3.0. Urban Sciences, PPEL, and Criminology all require it, and 3.13 isn't a big cushion.
  • Use tutoring in week two, not week ten.

2. Emails worth sending, and send them yourself

  • Urban Sciences: vniblett@dcp.ufl.edu — ask whether AP credit satisfies the macroeconomics and American Government prerequisites, and what would make you a stronger applicant.
  • PPEL / Hamilton: hamilton-advising@ufl.edu — same AP question, plus how competitive the applicant pool tends to be.
  • Liberal arts (Criminology, English): drop-in Zoom advising six times a week, no appointment needed. See the schedule.

3. Picking spring classes in November

  • If you're aiming at Urban Sciences, PPEL, Criminology or English, you don't need anything specific. Pick classes that keep you at three or four total.
  • US History since 1877 is the single highest-value elective. It's the only thing standing between you and all four journalism majors.
  • Public Speaking counts as an approved elective inside the Urban Sciences degree, so it does double duty.
  • Survey of Calculus is the only way to reach Business, Economics, or Food and Resource Economics. It isn't needed for anything else on this list.
  • Three or four classes keeps your Pell Grant money up. Two classes cuts it roughly in half.

4. Worth knowing before choosing

  • UF lets transfer students apply to one main campus major per term, and says changing majors afterward is highly unlikely. The major you apply to is realistically the one you graduate in.
  • UF Online is a separate application, so you could apply to one campus major and one online program in the same cycle.
  • Prerequisite courses cannot be taken at UF after admission. Everything has to be finished first.
