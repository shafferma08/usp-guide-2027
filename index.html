Being strategic about UF

The goal is to apply where you have a real shot, not just where the name sounds right.

You get two shots per cycle, not one

  • UF lets a transfer student apply to one campus major per term, and switching majors after you arrive is very unlikely. So the campus major you apply to is the one you graduate in.
  • UF Online is a separate application. You can apply to one campus major and one UF Online major in the same cycle. UF Online degrees are full UF degrees, and the bars are usually lower.
  • The smart setup is one campus application where you have a real chance, plus one UF Online application as a solid backup.

UF recalculates your GPA on its own scale (a B+ counts 3.33, not 3.5), which puts you around 3.11 right now. Urban Sciences, PPEL, Criminology on campus, Sustainability, and the new Hamilton majors all need a 3.0. Here is roughly where you land after College Algebra and Managerial Accounting:

  • AMH 2020 + SPC 2608 + SYG 2000. That combination opens every journalism major, Tourism (campus and online), and African American Studies, on top of everything you already qualify for. It roughly doubles your options.

  • Three or four classes keeps your Pell Grant money up. Two classes cuts it roughly in half.

  • Liberal Arts (CLAS) wants at least half of a major's prerequisites finished with grades when it reviews your application. Spring classes will still be in progress at the May 1 deadline.
