Kai's plan

Two applications. Advertising on campus is the one campus major. Sport Management through UF Online is a separate backup. Neither chosen major needs Survey of Calculus.

  1. Campus application: Advertising in the College of Journalism and Communications. It is the journalism-school major closest to marketing. UF lets you apply to one campus major per term, and switching later is unlikely.
    • CJC transfer admission, for every major including Advertising, requires a Florida AA, a 2.0 GPA (about 2.5 is competitive), and these five courses with a C or better: ENC 1102, AMH 2020, POS 2041, STA 2023, and ECO 2013. For Advertising, PSY 2012 is accepted in place of POS 2041. CJC transfer handout
    • AMH 2020 is the one missing admission course. You already have ENC 1102, STA 2023, AP Macroeconomics (ECO 2013 at UF with a 3 or higher), and AP U.S. Government (POS 2041 at UF with a 3 or higher). Register for AMH 2020 in Spring 2027 and earn at least a C.
    • SPC 2608 is suggested, not required for admission. The major still requires public speaking with a C. The handout also accepts SPC 1608. Take SPC 2608 at Santa Fe. The catalog lists SPC 2608 or ORI 2000 on the Advertising critical-tracking list. UF catalog
    • Taken at UF: MMC 1009, ADV 3008, ENC 3252, and MAR 3023. Those are on the catalog critical-tracking list. They are not missing Santa Fe courses.
    • The college also requires foreign-language proficiency, on top of UF's transfer rule. Finish the beginning sequence of one language with a C or better, or pass a proficiency exam. The other path is the Quantitative Option. Either can be finished at UF. Your July 1 audit already marks UF's separate transfer foreign-language rule as MET. Language or Quantitative Option
  2. Backup application: Sport Management through UF Online. Same UF degree, separate application, and you can send it in the same term as the campus one.
    • Admission prerequisites are complete. That is an economics course plus ACG 2001 and ACG 2011, each with a C or better, and a 2.2 in those courses. Your class list shows ACG 2001 as an A and ACG 2011 as a B+, and you have microeconomics. The page says ACG 2001 plus ACG 2011 may count as ACG 2021. You also need a 2.0 overall. You do not need Survey of Calculus for Sport Management or for Advertising. UF Online Sport Management
    • Public speaking for this online major is a degree-plan course. The catalog puts SPC 2608 or AEC 3030C in semester 3 and leaves it off the critical-tracking list. The six critical-tracking courses named there are ECO 2013 or ECO 2023, MAC 1105, STA 2023, MAN 3025, MAR 3023, and PSY 2012. UF catalog
    • Campus Sport Management is the hard version of this major. Students who get those spots usually have about a 3.5, which is why the online major is the one to apply to.
    • UF Online deadlines for 2027: January 25 for summer and April 26 for fall. Deadline page
  3. Spring 2027 at Santa Fe
    • AMH 2020, the missing Advertising admission course
    • SPC 2608, public speaking for the major
    • PSY 2012 General Psychology
    • MAC 1105 only if you did not pass it. ACG 2011 is a B+, so it is not a spring retake. If College Algebra passed, take a first semester of a foreign language instead. The journalism college's language requirement, or its Quantitative Option, can be finished at UF.
  4. To-dos
    • AA graduation application already filed.
    • Send your official AP scores to UF.
    • Switch your advisement track off Business Admin.
